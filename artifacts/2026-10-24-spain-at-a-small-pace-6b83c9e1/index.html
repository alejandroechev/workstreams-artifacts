The grown-up logistics

Less rushing. More Spain.

Where to stay, without a car

Madrid: a lift-equipped apartment around Retiro / Ibiza or the quieter edges of Las Letras. The latter is central but street noise varies. Do not choose a hotel just because it is beside Chamartín: check the actual station on each ticket.

Valencia: around Colón / Eixample for the centre and buses to the science complex. Barcelona: Eixample near a metro lift; Sagrada Família or Passeig de Gràcia areas work well. An apartment kitchen and a separate sleeping area matter more than a perfect view.

The three-city route uses Madrid as its start/end anchor, not a nightly base. Avoid Valencia or Barcelona as return day trips with young children.

Trains, tickets & getting around

Reserve intercity seats together. Madrid–Valencia: allow 1 h 50–2 h 15 on the train; Madrid–Barcelona 2 h 30–3 h 15; Valencia–Barcelona about 3–4 h, depending on service. These are planning ranges, not verified February 2027 departures. Station access, security, snacks, and arrival transfers make the day longer.

Reach high-speed stations 35–45 minutes before departure. Madrid departures may use Chamartín or Atocha; Valencia services may use Joaquín Sorolla or another station during works. Confirm the printed ticket. Use official Renfe, iryo, or OUIGO sites, and check luggage, child-seat rules and fare conditions.

Renfe generally offers under-4 travel without a seat at no charge, with the required child ticket; a child who needs a seat requires the appropriate fare. Other operators differ. Do not assume a child travels free on every metro system. Check lifts and stroller access before each trip; buses can be easier than deep metro transfers.

Winter, playgrounds & realistic walking

Plan one main attraction, one optional outing and a 13:30–15:30 reset. Walking figures are outdoor planning caps, not measured routes; indoor museums add steps. Use a compact stroller and shorten the afternoon first. Every daily transfer is an estimate, not live routing.

Pack layers, rain covers, spare clothes and warm hats. Madrid can be cold near freezing in the morning; coastal cities can be windy and wet. Beaches are for a short promenade, not swimming. Retiro's Crystal Palace may be closed for works: the plan only assumes an exterior walk. Gulliver Park can close in rain or for maintenance.

Early March: Valencia needs a quieter plan

Fallas builds through late February, with extremely loud fireworks and crowd disruption; extra events can occur before March. The usual daily mascletà is 14:00, 1–19 March, at Plaza del Ayuntamiento. Avoid that area around showtime with little children; hearing protection does not eliminate the risk. Exact 2027 programmes are not confirmed here. Use the Turia or science-complex area instead and expect transport changes.
