Five ways to present a CI that fixes itself

The same story, five presentation strategies: a pipeline watchdog notices red builds, hands the evidence to a coding agent, and the agent either ships a fix or explains why it's not our bug. Pick the one that fits the room.

01 · STORY

The Night Shift

A live replay of one real overnight incident, minute by minute: red phone tests at 4 a.m., a misleading error, and a merged fix by lunch.

narrativeauto-play~4 min
 02 · GAME

You Are the Watchdog

The audience triages real failure logs: send to the agent, or skip as infrastructure? Two cards are traps that fooled humans too.

interactiveaudience vote~5 min
 03 · SIMULATION

Mission Control

Watch a simulated month of pipelines turn red and green, with the watchdog routing each failure. Flip the filters on and see the noise vanish.

live dashboardbefore / after~3 min
 04 · DATA STORY

Signal From Noise

A scroll-driven funnel: from every job the agent was handed down to the fixes that shipped, and how we cut the agent's workload by two thirds without losing one fix.

scrollytellingnumbers~4 min
 05 · MYSTERY

The Case of the Forbidden Process

A detective case file. The room interrogates witnesses, weighs evidence and accuses a suspect before the agent's verdict is revealed.

whodunitaudience accuses~5 min
