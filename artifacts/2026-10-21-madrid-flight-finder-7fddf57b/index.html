A real snapshot, not a prediction

Researched on October 21, 2026, approximately 13:47–13:56 UTC. Google Flights was set to round trip, economy, four adults, CLP, and nonstop on both legs. 633 unique date pairs were read from its price graph across 12–17-day departure spans; these cover February 1 through May 31 with the return flight departing by May 31.

Why 17 days? A 16-night stay can require a 17-day flight-date gap when the outbound arrives the following day. A 12-day gap can be too short. Actual nights are stated only for checked itineraries; other rows show a possible range, not a confirmed hotel stay.

The calendar is an estimate from Google Flights, not independently priced for every flight combination. Five shortlisted itineraries were opened through outbound selection, nonstop return selection and booking options. Only those show named operating airlines. Codeshare marketing labels are not treated as separate flights.

How certain are the prices?

Booking checked means Google Flights displayed the itinerary and an airline booking option at that total. No ticket was held or purchased. Calendar estimate means the price graph displayed that date-pair total. Inventory and final airline checkout were not independently confirmed. Fares can change, and this page does not refresh them.

Two adults and two children

Four adults were priced as permitted for this comparison. Per-person amounts are the total divided by four, not separate child-fare quotes. Enter the children's actual ages at booking; child discounts, seating rules and conditions can differ. No infant or lap-infant pricing is used.

Baggage, seats and refunds

Taxes and required fees are included as shown by Google Flights. Lowest fares generally exclude checked bags and paid seat selection. For the featured March trip, Iberia Basic is CLP 2,799,852; Optimal with one checked bag per passenger is CLP 3,192,252; Comfort with bags and seat selection is CLP 3,859,332; Flexible is CLP 4,134,012. These are totals for four. Google reports one carry-on per passenger. Seat placement together is not guaranteed by this comparison. LATAM checked-bag fees were not available; do not interpret them as zero.

Airline-site access and booking links

LATAM's official route page denied automated access, and Iberia's route page returned an interrupted-connection error. Therefore all quoted fares come from Google Flights, including airline-direct booking options where checked. Links open the exact itinerary or date search, not a guaranteed ticket price. Recheck passenger count, nonstop filter, baggage and operating airline before paying. No external scripts, trackers or live-price API are used here.

Reading dates correctly

Flight times are local at each airport. “+1” means arrival on the next calendar day. Return dates in the explorer are departures from Madrid, not arrivals home. Both flight departure dates fall within February–May; an overnight return at the end of May could arrive in Chile in June. Madrid nights are the difference between local arrival in Madrid and departure from Madrid; they are not a hotel reservation or a count of full sightseeing days.
