The two-hour rule needs a real itinerary. Valladolid and Albacete are conditional candidates, not verified sub-two-hour journeys from Sol. Published route summaries quote about 1h43 and 1h53 respectively using Madrid mainline stations; add your journey from Sol, boarding time, waits and the final stadium approach. Budget roughly 1h50-2h20 for Valladolid and 2h05-2h35 for Albacete from Sol. If two hours is a hard limit, use Madrid region only until a date-specific route proves otherwise.

Allow an additional 45-60 minutes for stadium entry. Check the last return train after the final whistle and post-match crowds; an evening game may require an overnight stay. Fast trains are reserved-seat intercity services, not covered by an ordinary Madrid metro ticket.

Ticket prices, availability and buying safely

Every range labelled planning allowance is an editorial budgeting estimate for one adult standard seat, not a published minimum/maximum or an offer. Seat location, demand, membership, fees and match category can put the actual price outside it. Unknown official prices are explicitly left unknown. Hospitality is separate and is not included in the saved-list budget.

Real Madrid-Athletic advertises VIP from €450 and says general sale is coming soon. This was read in the rendered official match page; we did not reserve a seat or verify checkout inventory. Valladolid-Sporting displays LET ME KNOW for both general sale and VIP; the VIP teaser starts at €90 but is not a purchasable offer at this check.

Leganés and Albacete's rendered ticket portals did not list the February match. The Real Madrid women's portal exposed no selectable February event. Atlético and Madrid CFF blocked automated access; Rayo's ticket notice was also blocked. Unknown does not mean sold out, and absence from the portal is not proof of no future sale.

Use club-linked sellers only. Rayo's link is a club ticket-information route, not a verified online checkout; follow the match announcement for the official sales channel or stadium box-office instructions. Castilla's published ticket FAQ restricts sales to Real Madrid socios; a tourist or ordinary Madridista membership should not be assumed eligible. Never buy a transferred personal member ticket on that assumption.

Do not buy non-refundable transport until the kickoff is official. Seat maps and checkout determine actual availability and adjacent seating. Real Madrid requires an individual ticket for every spectator, including children, and its online-ticket instructions require mobile entry. Check each venue's own child, accessibility and bag rules.
