Welcome to the workshop

Nothing is quite
what it seems.

The colored pieces are already in the right places. Your job is to make their cast shadows look like the target using the camera and, later, the lights.

  1. Compare the two silhouettes. Match position, shape, size, and darkness. The match meter measures all four; filling the frame with darkness will not work.
  2. Move one control at a time. Orbit the puzzle camera, then learn to tilt it, move lights, and eventually create a second light.
  3. Inspect the real 3D scene. Drag the dark workshop to see how the pieces and rays line up. This is a separate inspection camera, not a puzzle move.
  4. Reach the goal, then seal your silhouette. Earn three stars by exceeding the goal by 9 points, two by 4, or one for clearing it. Tracing or a hint caps stars at two; a nudge caps them at one. Replay for a cleaner solve.

Later, two lights make two partly transparent shadows. Their overlap is darker. You must match both the silhouette and these tones.

Keyboard: Tab to any slider, then use arrow keys for fine adjustments. Focus the live shadow and use Left/Right for orbit, Up/Down for tilt once unlocked. Focus the workshop and use arrows to inspect.
Stuck? The first hint points you in a useful direction. Further hints gently nudge one control. There is no timer or move limit.
