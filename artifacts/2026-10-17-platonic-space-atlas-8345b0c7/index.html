How to interpret this atlas

Families and cross-links are editorial aids, not a claim that the hypothesized space has these coordinates or boundaries. "Why this belongs" explains a proposed membership or the relevance of a critique. An organism is an embodiment; the candidate form, competency, or relation is the putative pattern.

Claim labels describe the cited discussion, not independent replication or scientific consensus. "Formal" does not mean that a separate Platonic realm has been proved. Reported biological or computational results do not establish disembodied agency, consciousness, or a nonphysical cause. Critiques remain visible alongside the proposals they challenge.

Read from available English captions, mostly machine-generated. Transcription and timestamp alignment can be imperfect; the atlas does not analyze visuals, verify every paper, or reproduce full transcripts. Missing videos contribute no inferred ideas. Clinical examples are research reports, not medical advice.

No analytics, external scripts, embedded videos, or network requests are needed to explore. YouTube opens only when you follow a source link. "Copy link" uses your browser clipboard only when requested.
