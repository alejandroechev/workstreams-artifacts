What “source-checked” means, and how ranking works

Research covered company career pages, Ashby, Greenhouse, Lever, Workable, Get on Board, Built In, We Work Remotely, and specialist boards. Search-engine summaries were used for discovery, never as proof of eligibility. US-only, incompatible country lists, hybrid-only roles, closed applications, and duplicated city versions were excluded from the main list.

Employer / ATS means a company page or applicant-tracking system. Employer posting means a live employer application hosted by Get on Board. Board lead means a third-party listing without confirmed employer application status. These leads, one location-unclear employer role, and a project network are hidden by default. No application was submitted and no recruiter was contacted.

Location labels preserve the narrower published restriction. A region such as Latin America includes Chile geographically; it is not proof of payroll, tax, visa, or contractor eligibility. “Hours not published” means exactly that, not asynchronous work. Confirm scheduling, employment arrangement, English requirements, on-call expectations, travel, and pay applicability directly.

Agentic relevance is an editorial ordering, not a suitability score: production agent systems and agent-led development first, evaluation work next, AI-adjacent engineering next, then broader software. Within a group, confirmed sources precede leads and known recent dates precede unknown dates. Dates mean posted or republished as shown by the source, not necessarily a newly funded requisition. Unknown dates stay unknown; ATS update timestamps are not treated as posting dates.

Summaries are original notes, not copies of full job descriptions. Listings can close without notice. Bookmarks stay only in this browser under a page-specific key; nothing is sent to a server. External sources have their own policies. There are no trackers, external scripts, auto-apply tools, or live API calls in this page.
