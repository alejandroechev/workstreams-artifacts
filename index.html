Artifact gallery

  • Madrid in February 2027 — A Weather Almanac — an interactive calendar of historical daily low/high temperature distributions and wet-day probabilities, based on 30 years of ERA5 data, with percentile ranges, histograms, Celsius/Fahrenheit, and CSV export.
  • Madrid Matchdays — 12–21 February 2027 — source-checked football fixtures, official ticket links and availability caveats, women's and reserve-team games, a stadium map, and conditional fast-rail day trips.
  • Iberia Santiago–Madrid February 2027 Family Fare Explorer — a clickable calendar of Iberia night nonstops for two adults and two children, with fare families, checked-bag and seats-together costs, real A350 seat maps, a change/cancellation cost simulator, and Puerto Montt–Santiago connecting flights ranked by price (in Spanish).
  • WikiQuest — A little curiosity goes a long way — 80 Wikipedia-sourced trivia questions across 70s rock, world soccer, flags and countries, and 20th-century events, with increasing difficulty and three limited-use lifelines.
  • Solitaire Garden — Un ratito de solitario — six classic card games in Spanish, with a simple selection grid, large cards, hints, undo, and saved progress. No ads, timers, or external dependencies.
  • Spain, at a small pace — three car-free, winter family itineraries with a linked 14-day calendar and location map, realistic train-transfer budgets, rest breaks, and rainy-day alternatives.
  • Lisa See Spanish Book Cart — select nine Spanish editions on Amazon, compare Spanish/English ratings, track the US$49 free-shipping threshold, and estimate Chilean import costs.
  • Lisa See Book Buying Guide — compare Spanish and English editions across Chilean and overseas bookstores, with shipping, Chilean VAT, source links, and a multi-book cost calculator.
  • Santiago to Madrid Flight Finder — compare 633 nonstop date pairs for February–May 2027, with checked four-passenger itineraries, fare filters, and a departure-price calendar.
  • Curve Studio — compare four space-filling traversals, tune animated backgrounds, and export a standalone renderer.
  • Platonic Space Atlas — a source-linked, interactive mind map of possible forms, living patterns, minds, and competing interpretations.
